> About the association

CyberJU builds practical skills in IT and cybersecurity outside the lecture hall. We organize CTF competitions, hackathons and workshops. We're also proud to have placed in the top 5 in SäkerhetsSM.


[!] No prior experience required. We welcome all students, from complete beginners to experts.

A CTF is a type of competition where you solve technical challenges to find a hidden text string (the "flag"). It's the fastest way to learn applied IT security.
